## ✨ New Extension: Agentforce Vibes Auto Complete

Introducing the new Auto Complete extension for Agentforce Vibes. As you write Apex and LWC (JavaScript, CSS, and HTML), AI-powered suggestions appear as ghost text directly in your editor, helping you complete code faster without switching to chat. Stay focused, reduce repetitive typing, and keep your development flow uninterrupted.
